Our Story

Built Different.
Built to Last.

THOUS — The House of Uncle Smuzi — was founded on a simple belief: that the best strategies are built at the intersection of clarity and creativity. We don't do generic. We don't do copy-paste. Every engagement is shaped around the specific intelligence of the people in the room.

We work with founders, teams, and organisations who are tired of surface-level advice and ready for operational depth. Whether you're scaling a startup, restructuring a legacy business, or building something entirely new — we bring the frameworks, the rigour, and the perspective to help you build what lasts.

"Design intent meets reality. That's where the real work happens."
