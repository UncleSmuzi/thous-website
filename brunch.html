The HOUS Brunch

Good food. Better conversation. Real connections.

The Gathering

Come to the Table

A monthly gathering for people who are building something that matters. No pitches. No performances. Just real conversation with people who get it.

What to Expect

Food, Flow & Forward Motion

The HOUS Brunch is not a networking event. It is a gathering. We sit, we eat, we talk about what we are actually building — and what is actually in the way.

  • Intentionally small (20–30 people)
  • Curated seating — no cliques, no hierarchies
  • One conversation prompt to start, then let it flow
  • Builders, creators, operators, founders
  • Hosted at venues that understand the assignment
